# Knob Repair

Replace or repair the Atom Amp 2 volume knob.

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Time:** 1 minute  
**Tools:** 1/16" hex head driver

---

<div class="repair-step">
  <div class="repair-step-media"></div>
  <div class="repair-step-content">
    <div class="repair-step-header">
      <span class="repair-step-number">1</span>
      <h3 class="repair-step-title">Disconnect Power</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Unplug the power cable from your Atom Amp 2</li>
      <li>Disconnect any audio cables (RCA, headphones)</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="repair-warning">
      Always disconnect power before servicing the device to avoid electrical shock or damage.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="repair-step">
  <div class="repair-step-media">
    <img src="/img/atom-amp-2/knob-repair-1.JPG" alt="Locate the volume knob set screw" />
  </div>
  <div class="repair-step-content">
    <div class="repair-step-header">
      <span class="repair-step-number">2</span>
      <h3 class="repair-step-title">Align and Tighten the Set Screw</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Ensure the knob set screw is aligned with the flat spot of the potentiometer</li>
      <li>Tighten moderately to the shaft without stripping the head or threads on the screw</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
